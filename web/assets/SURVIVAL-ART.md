# Survival interface assets

The user's supplied dashboard image is the visual reference. These are decorative assets for the working application, not replacements for the calibrated Gateway map.

Generated on 2026-11-13 with the built-in `image_gen` tool, following the imagegen skill. Originals were copied into this project; the runtime never needs the generated-images directory.

## Jungle backdrop: `survival-jungle.png`

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic background artwork for a dinosaur survival radar web application, not a mockup. A very wide panoramic Jurassic tropical jungle at dusk. An active distant volcano slightly left of center beneath smoke and storm clouds, subtle orange glowing lava. Dense dark emerald ferns and broad leaves framing both sides and the bottom, two small realistic theropod dinosaur silhouettes on a distant ridge to the right, humid fog, ancient wild atmosphere. Detailed realistic game environment concept art with weathered forest greens, near-black olive shadows, restrained antique golden sunlight and warm volcanic orange. Composition must work as a thin website header crop from the upper half and as a low contrast page backdrop. Center horizon, panoramic landscape, requested aspect ratio about 2:1, high quality. No writing, no lettering, no logos, no user interface, no borders, no maps. Keep it dark enough for cream white text overlaid by real HTML, retain readable environmental detail rather than pure black.

## Portrait atlas: `survival-dinosaurs.png`

Generated with a transparent background. CSS uses the left half for Allosaurus and the right half for Carnotaurus. Other species use the SVG skull. These illustrations are indicative species artwork.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent two-cell dinosaur portrait sprite for a survival game website. A wide 2:1 image consisting of TWO equally sized square cells with a completely transparent background. LEFT HALF: one realistic Allosaurus head and upper neck, side view looking right, olive brown reptile scales, characteristic subtle small brow crests above the eyes, powerful open jaw, amber eye. RIGHT HALF: one realistic Carnotaurus head and upper neck, side view looking right, recognizable two distinct short horns above the eyes, rugged reddish brown scales, orange eye, slightly open jaws. Both portraits at the same scale, entirely contained in their own half without any overlapping into the other half, leave a small transparent margin around each. AAA dinosaur survival game concept art, detailed leathery scales, dramatic antique gold rim lighting from upper left, soft teal light in the shadows, realistic anatomy and muted earthy palette. This is an isolated game art asset, no writing, no lettering, no captions, no logos, no borders, no panels, no frames, no UI, no extra creatures. Clean transparent alpha behind BOTH heads.

## Native assets

- `survival-icons.svg`: project-native SVG symbol sprite for navigation, status, measures and the skull emblem.
- `survival-metal.svg`: project-native SVG grain and scratch texture. No raster editing was used.
- `barlow-condensed-semibold.ttf`: Barlow Condensed Semibold, bundled from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed). The SIL Open Font License is bundled as `barlow-condensed-OFL.txt`.
